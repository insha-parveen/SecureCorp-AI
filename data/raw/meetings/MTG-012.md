# Meeting Summary
The Q1 Operations Review confirmed strong growth and successful delivery of the ACME project. However, it highlighted a critical risk: the gap between the high demand for hybrid retrieval and the current latency bottlenecks. Resource constraints in the APAC region were identified as a scaling risk, and the failures from the DR simulation emphasized the need for better search index replication.

# Decisions Taken
1. **Resource Strategy:** Prioritize internal transfers from BLR to Singapore to scale the APAC delivery team.
2. **Latency Management:** Transition from "selling speed" to "selling accuracy" until the Orion Beta semantic cache is stable.
3. **DR Compliance:** Implement a mandatory check for both dense and sparse index replication across all sites.

# Open Issues
1. **APAC Headcount:** The current budget for new hires in Singapore is not yet approved by Finance.
2. **SLA Breach:** The latency for ACME is still technically in breach of the SLA.
