# Code of Conduct

## 1. Introduction
NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is built on a foundation of trust, integrity, and professional excellence. Our Code of Conduct serves as the ethical compass for every team member, from entry-level associates to senior executives. It defines the standards of behavior we expect from each other and from our interactions with clients, vendors, and the public. Adherence to this code is not merely a suggestion; it is a fundamental condition of employment.

## 2. Professional Behavior and the Respectful Workplace

### 2.1 Core Behavioral Standards
We believe that a diverse, inclusive, and respectful environment is the primary catalyst for innovation. Every employee is expected to:
- **Treat Others with Dignity:** Interact with all colleagues, clients, and partners with respect, regardless of their role, seniority, gender, ethnicity, religion, age, or physical ability.
- **Professionalism:** Maintain a professional demeanor in all business interactions. This includes punctuality, appropriate attire (relative to the client environment), and polished communication.
- **Positive Collaboration:** Foster a culture of support. We value those who help others succeed and who contribute to a positive team atmosphere.

### 2.2 Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination
NexaCore maintains a zero-tolerance policy for any form of harassment, bullying, or discrimination.
- **Definition of Harassment:** This includes any unwelcome conduct—verbal, physical, or visual—that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment.
- **Examples of Prohibited Behavior:** Unwelcome sexual advances, derogatory comments about a person's identity, persistent belittling of a colleague's work, or any form of physical intimidation.
- **Reporting:** Any employee who witnesses or experiences harassment is encouraged and expected to report it immediately. Refer to the **Workplace Complaint and Grievance Procedure (HR-008)** for the formal reporting channels.

## 3. Ethical Business Practices

### 3.1 Conflicts of Interest
A conflict of interest occurs when an employee's private interests interfere—or appear to interfere—with the interests of NexaCore or its clients.
- **Common Conflict Scenarios:**
    - Holding a financial interest in a direct competitor or a vendor we are currently evaluating.
    - Using company time or resources to run a side business that competes with NexaCore.
    - Hiring or promoting a family member or close personal friend without full disclosure to HR.
- **Disclosure Requirement:** Employees must disclose any potential or actual conflict of interest to their Reporting Manager and the HR department upon joining and whenever a new conflict arises.
- **Resolution Process:** Once a conflict is disclosed, the company will determine if it can be managed (e.g., by removing the employee from a specific client account) or if it must be eliminated to avoid a breach of trust.

### 3.2 Gifts, Entertainment, and Bribery
To maintain absolute objectivity in our business decisions, employees must not accept gifts, favors, or entertainment that could be perceived as an attempt to influence a business outcome.
- **Nominal Gifts:** Small, low-value items (e.g., corporate branded calendars, notebooks, or pens) are generally acceptable.
- **Significant Gifts:** Any gift with a value exceeding ₹5,000 must be disclosed to the Reporting Manager and HR. The company may require the gift to be returned or donated to a charity.
- **Prohibited Items:** Cash, gift cards, stocks, or lavish trips are strictly prohibited, regardless of the value or the relationship with the giver.
- **Anti-Bribery:** NexaCore strictly prohibits the offering or acceptance of bribes. This includes "facilitation payments" to government officials or vendors to expedite a process.

## 4. Information Handling and Confidentiality

### 4.1 Protecting Company and Client Assets
Employees are entrusted with NexaCore's intellectual property and our clients' most sensitive data.
- **Physical Assets:** Company equipment (laptops, mobile devices) must be used solely for business purposes and protected from theft or damage.
- **Digital Assets:** Unauthorized duplication, distribution, or sale of company source code, architectural diagrams, or internal strategy documents is a severe violation of this code.

### 4.2 Confidentiality and Data Privacy
As a technology partner, our reputation depends on our ability to keep secrets.
- **Client Confidentiality:** All client information, from project requirements to internal systems, is strictly confidential. Discussing client work in public spaces or with non-authorized personnel is prohibited.
- **Internal Confidentiality:** Company financial records, payroll details, and strategic roadmaps must not be shared externally.
- **Data Privacy:** Employees must handle personal data in accordance with the company's data privacy guidelines and the local laws of the regions we operate in (e.g., GDPR-like standards for European clients).

For the technical requirements of data protection, refer to the **Information Security Policy (ITSEC-001)**.

## 5. Acceptable Technology Use

### 5.1 Digital Communication Standards
Our communication tools (Email, Slack, Teams) are professional environments.
- **Appropriateness:** Avoid using these channels for personal politics, religious debates, or any content that could be deemed offensive or unprofessional.
- **Tone:** Maintain a respectful and constructive tone, even during disagreements.
- **Expectation of Privacy:** Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company-owned devices and networks. NexaCore reserves the right to monitor these systems for security, legal compliance, and policy enforcement.

### 5.2 Social Media and Public Presence
While we respect an employee's right to a personal social media presence, that presence must not compromise NexaCore's reputation.
- **The Disclaimer:** When mentioning NexaCore or the technology industry on social media, employees should clearly state that their views are their own and do not represent the company.
- **Prohibitions:** Never post confidential client information, internal company secrets, or disparaging comments about colleagues or clients on public platforms.

## 6. Reporting Misconduct and the Whistleblower Policy

### 6.1 The Duty to Report
Maintaining an ethical workplace is a collective responsibility. Employees who become aware of any breach of this Code of Conduct have a professional obligation to report it. Silence in the face of misconduct is considered a failure of professional responsibility.

### 6.2 Reporting Channels
Complaints can be filed through several channels, depending on the severity and nature of the issue:
- **Direct Reporting:** To the employee's **Reporting Manager** (unless the manager is the subject of the complaint).
- **HR Reporting:** Directly to the HR department or a designated HR representative.
- **Anonymous Reporting:** Through the company's secure whistleblower portal for reporting fraud, severe harassment, or systemic corruption.

The detailed investigation and resolution process is found in the **Workplace Complaint and Grievance Procedure (HR-008)**.

### 6.3 Non-Retaliation Guarantee
NexaCore strictly prohibits any form of retaliation against an employee who reports a concern in good faith. Retaliation—including demotion, social isolation, or negative performance reviews—will be treated as a severe violation of the Code of Conduct and may result in immediate termination.

## 7. Consequences of Non-Compliance
Violations of the Code of Conduct are addressed based on the severity and intent of the breach. Possible disciplinary actions include:
- **Verbal Warning:** For first-time, minor infractions.
- **Written Warning:** A formal record of a violation, often accompanied by a required corrective action.
- **Suspension:** Temporary removal from duties, with or without pay, during an investigation.
- **Demotion:** Removal from a leadership role or a reduction in grade.
- **Termination:** Immediate end of employment for gross misconduct or repeated violations.
