# Performance Review Policy

## 1. Purpose
NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is committed to a culture of high performance, accountability, and continuous professional growth. We believe that regular, structured feedback is the most effective way to align individual contributions with company objectives and to help our employees reach their full potential. The Performance Review Policy provides the framework for evaluating employee contributions, fostering development, and ensuring a fair and consistent approach to performance management.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees of NexaCore Solutions. Contract employees may be subject to a simplified review process as defined in their specific contracts.

## 3. The Performance Review Cycle
NexaCore utilizes a dual-cycle review system. This approach combines a tactical mid-year course correction with a comprehensive end-of-year evaluation.

### 3.1 The Annual Review (The Primary Cycle)
The Annual Review is the formal end-of-year evaluation that assesses an employee's total contribution over the previous 12 months.
- **Timing:** The cycle begins in March and concludes by March 31, coinciding with the end of the Indian fiscal year.
- **Objective:** To provide a definitive performance rating, review the achievement of annual KPIs, and determine suitability for promotions or salary adjustments.
- **Outcome:** The result of the Annual Review is a final rating that is recorded in the employee's permanent record in the HR portal.

### 3.2 The Semi-Annual Check-in (The Tactical Cycle)
The Semi-Annual Check-in is designed as a less formal, more agile review focusing on immediate goals and development.
- **Timing:** Occurs every September.
- **Objective:** To review progress against mid-year goals, identify any blockers, and adjust targets for the remainder of the fiscal year.
- **Value:** These check-ins prevent "surprises" during the Annual Review by ensuring that employees and managers are in constant alignment regarding expectations.

## 4. Goal Setting and Performance Indicators

### 4.1 Collaborative Goal Setting
At the beginning of each fiscal year (April), every employee and their **Reporting Manager** must collaborate to set a set of a specific, measurable, and ambitious goals.
- **The SMART Framework:** All goals must be Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.
- **Strategic Alignment:** Individual goals must not only be technical but must map directly to the objectives of the employee's department and the broader NexaCore strategic vision.
- **Documentation:** All finalized goals must be entered into the Performance Management module of the HR portal by April 30th.

### 4.2 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Depending on the role, KPIs may include:
- **Engineering:** Code quality (defect rate), on-time delivery of features, system uptime, and contribution to internal libraries.
- **Sales:** Revenue targets, new client acquisitions, and pipeline growth.
- **IT and Security:** Number of resolved security vulnerabilities, system availability, and ticket resolution time.
- **General:** Adherence to the **Code of Conduct (HR-006)**, team collaboration, and mentorship of junior staff.

### 4.3 Dynamic Goal Adjustment
Goals are not static. If project scopes change, a client contract is terminated, or new business priorities emerge, goals may be updated. Such updates must be documented in the HR portal and agreed upon by both the employee and the Reporting Manager.

## 5. The Review Process Step-by-Step

### 5.1 Employee Self-Review
The review process begins with a period of self-reflection. Every employee is required to complete a comprehensive self-review.
- **Achievement Log:** Employees should document their key achievements, highlighting specific projects where they added significant value.
- **Challenge Analysis:** Employees should identify the biggest challenges they faced and explain how they overcame them or what they learned.
- **Evidence-Based Claims:** Whenever possible, employees should provide evidence (e.g., links to merged PRs, client commendation emails, or completed certifications).

### 5.2 Managerial Evaluation
Following the self-review, the Reporting Manager conducts an independent evaluation of the employee's performance.
- **Holistic Assessment:** The manager considers not only the achievement of KPIs but also the employee's behavioral alignment with company values, their level of collaboration, and their impact on the team.
- **Calibration:** To ensure fairness across the organization, managers meet with their own supervisors and HR to "calibrate" ratings, ensuring that a "Meets Expectations" rating in Engineering is equivalent to one in Sales.

### 5.3 The Performance Review Meeting
A formal one-on-one meeting is scheduled to discuss the findings.
- **Dialogue Focus:** The meeting is a two-way conversation. Employees are encouraged to share their perspective on the manager's evaluation and discuss their career aspirations.
- **Agreement:** The goal is to reach a mutual agreement on the final rating and the goals for the coming period.

## 6. Rating Principles and Scale
NexaCore uses a standardized four-tier rating scale to ensure consistency.

| Rating | Descriptor | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds Expectations** | High Performer | Consistently delivers results far above the required standard; drives innovation; mentors others; takes ownership of complex problems. |
| **Meets Expectations** | Solid Performer | Consistently meets all key objectives and behavioral standards; reliable, professional, and produces high-quality work. |
| **Developing** | Growth Required | Making progress but requires more support, training, or guidance in certain key areas to reach full proficiency. |
| **Below Expectations** | Under-performer | Consistently fails to meet core objectives; lacks the necessary skills or effort; requires immediate and formal intervention. |

## 7. Professional Development and Performance Improvement

### 7.1 Professional Development Plans (PDP)
Every review cycle concludes with the creation of a PDP.
- **Skills Gap Analysis:** Identifying the delta between current skills and those required for the next career level.
- **Learning Path:** Specifying courses, certifications, or internal stretch projects to bridge that gap.
- **Support:** The company may provide budget for approved certifications or training courses as part of the PDP.

### 7.2 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
Employees who receive a "Below Expectations" rating may be placed on a formal PIP.
- **Purpose:** A PIP is a structured, time-bound opportunity (typically 30, 60, or 90 days) to improve performance to an acceptable level.
- **The Plan:** The PIP includes a set of very specific, non-negotiable targets and a schedule of weekly check-ins with the Reporting Manager.
- **Support:** The manager provides increased guidance and clear feedback on progress.
- **Consequences:** Failure to successfully meet the requirements of a PIP may result in further disciplinary action, including termination of employment.

## 8. Appeals and Clarification
NexaCore believes in a fair process. If an employee strongly disagrees with their final rating, they may request a "Rating Review" within 5 business days of the review meeting.
- **Process:** The request is submitted to the HR department.
- **Review Panel:** A panel consisting of the Head of HR and the manager's direct supervisor will review the evidence and the rating to ensure no bias occurred.

## 9. Confidentiality and Data Security
Performance reviews are highly sensitive and confidential.
- **Access Control:** Access is strictly limited to the employee, their Reporting Manager, the manager's direct supervisor, and the HR department.
- **Storage:** All records are stored in the secure Performance Management module of the HR portal.
- **Privacy:** Discussing another employee's rating or performance in a public or semi-public setting is a violation of the **Code of Conduct (HR-006)**.
