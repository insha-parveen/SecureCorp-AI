**16:00**  
**Sunita Rao:**  
This is the final gate for the Project Orion Beta. We've spent the last few weeks optimizing the reranker and the cache. Sarah, walk us through the final numbers.

**Sarah Jenkins:**

**Sarah Jenkins:**

**Sunita Rao:**  
Rohit, from the security side, are we a "Go"?

**16:17**  
**Sunita Rao:**  
Does that affect the production environment?

**Rohit Verma:**  
No. Production is clean. We're using a fresh set of IAM roles and a strictly controlled secret management process. The staging issue is an internal housekeeping task, not a production blocker. I'm comfortable signing off on the release.
