# Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish a fair, transparent, and consistent framework for managing employee time off at NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. We recognize that a balanced professional life is essential for long-term productivity and mental health. This policy ensures that employees can take necessary time for rest and recovery, illness, and personal obligations without compromising the operational stability of our client deliveries.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees of NexaCore Solutions across all global locations, including Lucknow (LKO), Bengaluru (BLR), Dubai (DXB), and Singapore (SIN). Part-time employees or contract staff are subject to the terms defined in their individual service agreements.

## 3. General Principles of Leave Management
- **Business Continuity:** While NexaCore supports an employee's right to take leave, approval is always subject to business requirements and project deadlines.
- **Fairness:** Leave is granted based on the principles of equity and the order of request, provided that project stability is maintained.
- **Authorized Channels:** All Leave requests, regardless of duration, must be submitted and tracked through the official HR portal.
- **Approval Authority:** The **Reporting Manager** is the primary authority for approving or denying Leave requests, as they have the most direct visibility into project timelines and team capacity. HR provides secondary oversight to ensure compliance with this policy and local labor laws.

## 4. Leave Categories and Entitlements

### 4.1 Annual Leave
Annual Leave is intended for rest, relaxation, and the pursuit of personal interests. It is an essential component of employee well-being.
- **Entitlement:** Every full-time employee is entitled to **18 days of Annual Leave per year**.
- **Accrual Basis:** Annual Leave is accrued on a monthly basis (1.5 days per month). 
- **Carry-forward Rule:** To encourage employees to take their rest, NexaCore allows a maximum of 5 days of unused Annual Leave to be carried forward into the next fiscal year. Any balance exceeding 5 days at the end of the fiscal year (March 31) will be forfeited.
- **Usage Increments:** Annual Leave may be requested in increments of half-days or full days.

### 4.2 Sick Leave
Sick Leave is provided to support employees during periods of illness, injury, or medical emergencies that prevent them from performing their professional duties.
- **Entitlement:** Every full-time employee is entitled to **10 days of Sick Leave per year**.
- **Usage Guidelines:** Sick Leave may be used for the employee's own health needs or for the care of an immediate family member in an emergency.
- **Medical Documentation:** To prevent abuse and ensure the health of our workforce, any Sick Leave exceeding 3 consecutive working days requires a valid medical certificate issued by a registered healthcare provider. This certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal upon the employee's return to work.
- **Unused Balance:** Unlike Annual Leave, Sick Leave does not carry forward to the next year and cannot be encashed.

### 4.3 Other Specialized Leave Types
In addition to the core entitlements, NexaCore provides the following specialized leave:
- **Maternity and Paternity Leave:** Provided to support new parents. The specific duration and terms are governed by the statutory requirements of the country where the employee is based (e.g., Indian Maternity Benefit Act for LKO/BLR staff).
- **Bereavement Leave:** Up to 3 days of paid leave for the loss of an immediate family member. This leave is granted upon the request of the employee and the confirmation of the Reporting Manager.
- **Unpaid Leave:** In exceptional circumstances where all other leave balances are exhausted, an employee may request unpaid leave. This requires joint approval from the Reporting Manager and the Head of HR.

## 5. Application and Approval Process

### 5.1 Request Submission Timelines
To avoid disruption to client services, employees must adhere to the following notification windows:
- **Short-Term Annual Leave (1-3 days):** A minimum of 1 week's (5 business days) notice is required.
- **Long-Term Annual Leave (4+ days):** A minimum of 2 weeks' notice is required. This allows the Reporting Manager to arrange coverage for the employee's responsibilities.
- **Sick Leave:** Notification must be provided to the Reporting Manager as soon as practically possible, ideally before the start of the employee's first scheduled working hour.

### 5.2 The Approval Workflow
1. **Submission:** The employee selects the date range and leave type in the HR portal and submits the request.
2. **Managerial Review:** The **Reporting Manager** evaluates the request against the current project sprint, upcoming client deadlines, and the leave status of other team members.
3. **Decision:** The Reporting Manager approves or denies the request. If denied, the manager should provide a brief justification (e.g., "Critical delivery phase for Project X").
4. **Notification:** The employee receives an automated alert from the portal regarding the decision.

### 5.3 Emergency Leave and Retrospective Filing
In the event of a sudden emergency (e.g., accident, sudden illness, family crisis), employees are expected to notify their Reporting Manager via phone or instant message immediately. Once the emergency is stabilized, the formal Leave request must be filed in the HR portal retrospectively within 48 hours of the employee's return to work.

## 6. Eligibility and Restrictions

### 6.1 Probationary Period
Employees joining NexaCore undergo a standard probation period.
- **Sick Leave:** Accessible immediately upon joining.
- **Annual Leave:** Accrued from day one, but cannot be utilized until the probation period is successfully completed. Exception: In cases of extreme emergency, the Head of HR may grant a one-time exception.

### 6.2 Blackout Periods and Critical Delivery Windows
NexaCore may designate specific "Blackout Periods" during which Annual Leave is generally restricted.
- **Examples:** The final two weeks of a major project delivery, the end-of-year financial audit, or a critical system migration.
- **Impact:** Leave requests during these periods are likely to be denied unless they are for emergency or medical reasons. The Reporting Manager will notify the team of any upcoming Blackout Periods at least 30 days in advance.

### 6.3 Leave Balance Management
- **Negative Balance:** Requesting leave beyond the accrued balance is generally not permitted. In very rare cases, "Advanced Leave" may be approved by HR, which will be deducted from future monthly accruals.
- **Payment in Lieu:** NexaCore does not offer payment in lieu of unused Annual Leave, except where specifically required by the local labor laws of the employee's jurisdiction.

## 7. Exceptions, Appeals, and Escalations

### 7.1 Exception Requests
If a Reporting Manager denies a Leave request and the employee believes there are extraordinary mitigating circumstances, the employee may submit a formal appeal to the HR department.

### 7.2 Conflict Resolution and Escalation Path
If a disagreement persists between an employee and their Reporting Manager regarding a Leave denial, the following escalation path must be followed:
1. **Manager's Manager:** The employee may request a review from the Reporting Manager's direct supervisor.
2. **HR Intervention:** If the issue remains unresolved, the matter will be escalated to the Head of HR for a final, binding decision.
