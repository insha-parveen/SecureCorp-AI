# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to define the rules and procedures for the reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred by employees of NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. We aim to provide a process that is fair, efficient, and transparent, ensuring that employees are not financially burdened by their professional duties while maintaining strict fiscal discipline and preventing the misuse of company funds.

By establishing clear boundaries on what is reimbursable and requiring a strict timeline for submission, NexaCore ensures that its financial records are accurate and that project budgets are updated in real-time.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees of NexaCore Solutions globally. It covers all out-of-pocket expenditures made by an employee on behalf of the company, ranging from travel and client meals to incidental operational costs.

## 3. General Principles of Reimbursement

### 3.1 The "Business Purpose" Requirement
For any expense to be reimbursable, it must have a clear and demonstrable business purpose. An expense is not reimbursable simply because it was incurred during working hours; it must be necessary for the achievement of a specific business objective (e.g., "Client meeting to discuss Q3 project roadmap").

### 3.2 Prudent Spending and Duty of Care
Employees are expected to exercise professional judgment and spend company funds as if they were their own. This means seeking the most cost-effective options that do not compromise the quality of the work or the professional image of NexaCore.
- **Reasonableness:** Expenses must be reasonable for the location and context.
- **Standard of Living:** While NexaCore supports a professional standard of travel and dining, lavish or extravagant spending is not permitted and may be rejected.

### 3.3 Evidence and Documentation
Reimbursement is an evidence-based process. No claim will be processed without valid, itemized proof of purchase. "Lost receipt" declarations are only permitted for nominal amounts under ₹200 and must be used sparingly.

## 4. Eligible and Non-Reimbursable Expenses

### 4.1 Travel-Related Expenses
For comprehensive guidelines on what is considered a reimbursable travel cost, employees must refer to the **Travel and Expense Policy (HR-004)**. 
- **Reimbursable items include:** Economy airfare, standard business hotels, approved ride-sharing/taxis, and per diem meals during travel.
- **Process:** All travel expenses must be claimed using the documentation requirements specified in HR-004, but the submission and approval flow follows this policy.

### 4.2 Client Engagement and Entertainment
Expenses incurred while hosting clients for business purposes are reimbursable, provided the following criteria are met:
- **Prior Approval:** Any client entertainment expense expected to exceed ₹5,000 must be pre-approved by the Reporting Manager.
- **Documentation:** The claim must include the names and roles of all attendees and a brief explanation of the business purpose of the meeting.
- **Reasonableness:** Costs must be reasonable and aligned with the lapped importance of the client relationship.

### 4.3 Operational and Incidental Expenses
Small, unplanned operational costs (e.g., a specific software tool for a project, urgent courier services) may be reimbursed.
- **Managerial Consent:** For any operational expense over ₹2,000, the employee should seek verbal or email consent from their Reporting Manager before making the purchase.
- **Exclusions:** General office supplies and hardware should be requested via the Procurement process (**FIN-001**) rather than through personal reimbursement.

### 4.4 Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following items are strictly non-reimbursable:
- **Personal Amenities:** Laundry, grooming, spa services, or toiletries.
- **Alcohol:** Alcohol is not reimbursable, except when it is a reasonable part of a pre-approved client entertainment event.
- **Legal Penalties:** Traffic fines, parking tickets, or other penalties incurred during business travel.
- **Guests:** Any costs associated with spouses, partners, or other non-employee guests.
- **Unsupported Claims:** Any expense where a valid, itemized receipt is missing.

## 5. The Reimbursement Process

### 5.1 Submission Timeline (The 30-Day Rule)
Timely submission is critical for accurate project billing and monthly financial closing.
- **Canonical Deadline:** All **Expense Reimbursement** claims must be submitted through the Finance portal **within 30 days of the expense date**.
- **Late Submissions:** Claims submitted after the 30-day window may be rejected.
- **Exception Process:** If there are exceptional mitigating circumstances (e.g., extended medical leave), the employee may submit an exception request. This request must be approved by their Department Head and reviewed by the Finance Manager.

### 5.2 Documentation Requirements
Every reimbursement request must be accompanied by:
1. **Itemized Receipts:** Digital scans of the original itemized receipt. A simple credit card statement is not a substitute for an itemized bill for expenses exceeding ₹1,000.
2. **Business Justification:** A clear description of why the expense was necessary and which project/cost center it should be billed to.
3. **Attendee Log:** For all meals or entertainment, a list of all participants.

### 5.3 Approval Workflow
1. **Submission:** The employee uploads all receipts and submits the claim in the Finance portal.
2. **Reporting Manager Approval:** The manager verifies the business purpose, the reasonableness of the cost, and that the expense was not already covered by a company-provided resource.
3. **Finance Audit:** The Finance department reviews the claim for policy compliance, verifies that the 30-day deadline was met, and checks for duplicate submissions.
4. **Payment Execution:** Once fully approved, the reimbursement is processed and paid in the next payroll cycle.

## 6. Rejections, Disputes, and Corrections

### 6.1 Grounds for Rejection
Finance may reject a claim in full or in part if:
- A receipt is missing, illegible, or fraudulent.
- The expense is clearly non-reimbursable under this policy.
- The claim is submitted after the 30-day deadline without an approved exception.
- The cost is deemed excessive or unreasonable for the given context.

### 6.2 Dispute Resolution
If an employee disagrees with a rejection, they may request a review.
- **Process:** The employee provides additional justification or documentation to the Finance Manager.
- **Final Decision:** The Finance Manager's decision on a reimbursement dispute is final.
