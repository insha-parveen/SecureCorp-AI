Hi Sarah,

I've had a chance to look at the preliminary Cloud Migration Plan.

While the technical approach is sound, I'm concerned about the remote access for the migration team. Since we'll be moving sensitive data, all migration activities must strictly adhere to the VPN & Remote Access Policy (ITSEC-008).

Specifically, we cannot use temporary public IPs for the migration bridge. We must use the secure corporate VPN with full audit logging enabled.

Please ensure this requirement is explicitly documented in ENG-002.
