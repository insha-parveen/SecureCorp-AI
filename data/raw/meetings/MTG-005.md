# Meeting Summary
The team planned the first sprint for the Cloud Migration project. The focus is on "Discovery and Baseline," with a heavy emphasis on identifying legacy data stores and dependencies, particularly within the Finance department. The technical setup will prioritize secure connectivity via the corporate VPN as per ITSEC-008.

# Decisions Taken
1. **Sprint 1 Goal:** Discovery and Baseline; prioritize identifying "shadow" data stores.
2. **Connectivity:** All migration traffic must be routed through the secure corporate VPN (ITSEC-008).
3. **Analysis Approach:** Use network traffic analysis and script reviews rather than relying solely on official registries.

# Open Issues
1. **Cutover Strategy:** The method for transitioning legacy scripts to the cloud (Proxy vs. Migration) is deferred to Sprint 2.
2. **Infrastructure Scale:** The full number of legacy servers is currently unknown and may impact the overall timeline.
