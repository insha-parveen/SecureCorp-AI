Hi Sarah,

I've reviewed the final draft of ENG-001 from a security perspective.

I have a concern regarding the data classification of the retrieved chunks. The current architecture doesn't explicitly state how the system will handle chunks with different classification levels (e.g., `public` vs `restricted`) within the same retrieval result set.

We must ensure that the authorization layer is applied *before* any evidence reaches the LLM. Please update the architecture to explicitly show the authorization check at the retrieval boundary.

Refer to ITSEC-006 (Data Classification Policy) for the specific roles required for each level.
