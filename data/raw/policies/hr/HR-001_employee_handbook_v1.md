# NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. — Employee Handbook

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Welcome to NexaCore
Welcome to NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. We are thrilled to have you join our team of innovators, consultants, and technologists. NexaCore is dedicated to providing world-class cloud solutions and managed IT services to our global clients. Our success is built on the expertise of our people and our commitment to delivering excellence in every project. This Employee Handbook is designed to introduce you to our culture, our expectations, and the policies that govern our professional environment.

### 1.2 Purpose of the Handbook
The purpose of this handbook is to provide a comprehensive overview of the terms and conditions of employment at NexaCore. It serves as a roadmap for your professional journey with us, outlining the rights, responsibilities, and standards we expect from every team member. This document is intended to be read in conjunction with your individual employment contract and the specialized policy documents maintained in the HR portal. Please note that this handbook provides a summary of policies; where specific procedures are required, this document will refer you to more detailed policy documents (e.g., HR-002 for Leave).

### 1.3 Nature of Employment
Employment with NexaCore is based on mutual trust and professional excellence. We operate as a dynamic consultancy, meaning our priorities can shift based on client needs and technological advancements. While we strive for long-term stability and growth for all our employees, the company reserves the right to modify policies, procedures, and organizational structures to meet business needs. Employment is generally at-will, subject to the specific terms of the legal jurisdiction in which the employee is hired.

## 2. Company Overview

### 2.1 Our Mission and Vision
NexaCore Solutions aims to bridge the gap between complex legacy infrastructure and the agile future of cloud computing. Our vision is to be the primary catalyst for digital transformation in the MEA and APAC regions. We strive to empower enterprises by reducing technical debt and implementing scalable, secure, and cost-effective cloud architectures.

### 2.2 Core Values
At NexaCore, we are guided by four core values:
- **Integrity:** We do the right thing, even when no one is watching. We are honest with our clients and transparent with our colleagues.
- **Innovation:** We don't just follow industry trends; we aim to set them. We encourage curiosity and the willingness to experiment with new technologies.
- **Client-Centricity:** Our clients' success is our success. We listen deeply to their needs and deliver solutions that solve real business problems.
- **Collaboration:** We believe the best solutions come from diverse perspectives. We break down silos and work across departments to achieve our goals.

### 2.3 Organizational Structure and Locations
NexaCore operates as a distributed organization with a strong presence in key technology hubs:
- **Lucknow (LKO):** Our global headquarters. This location serves as the central hub for our Administration, HR, and core Engineering teams. It is where our primary corporate governance and strategic planning occur.
- **Bengaluru (BLR):** Known as the "Silicon Valley of India," our Bengaluru office is a primary hub for high-scale Engineering and Sales, tapping into the region's deep talent pool.
- **Dubai (DXB):** Our gateway to the Middle East and Africa (MEA) region. This office focuses on high-touch Sales and Client Delivery, ensuring we meet the unique regulatory and business requirements of the Gulf region.
- **Singapore (SIN):** Our strategic APAC regional office, focusing on market expansion and regional client support in Southeast Asia.

### 2.4 Core Departments
Our operations are divided into the following departments, each with a distinct mandate:
- **Human Resources (HR):** Responsible for talent acquisition, onboarding, employee wellness, payroll administration, and policy compliance. HR ensures that NexaCore remains an employer of choice.
- **Engineering (ENG):** The heart of our delivery. This department includes cloud architects, software engineers, DevOps specialists, and QA analysts. They are responsible for designing and implementing the technical solutions our clients depend on.
- **Finance (FIN):** Manages the company's fiscal health. This includes corporate accounting, budgeting, tax compliance, and the processing of all Expense Reimbursements.
- **Operations (OPS):** Ensures the efficient delivery of managed services. Operations handles the logistical side of client engagement, resource allocation, and internal facility management.
- **Sales (SALES):** Responsible for revenue growth, market analysis, and managing the client relationship from initial contact to contract signing.
- **IT and Security (ITSEC):** Maintains the internal technology stack and implements the security frameworks that protect both corporate and client data. This department is led by the CISO.
- **Administration (ADMIN):** Oversees corporate governance, legal compliance, and general office operations.

## 3. Workplace Expectations

### 3.1 Professional Conduct
Every employee is expected to maintain a high standard of professionalism in all interactions. This is critical given our role as consultants who represent NexaCore to external clients.
- **Interpersonal Respect:** Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and dignity. We maintain a professional atmosphere where diverse opinions are welcomed.
- **Client Representation:** When interacting with clients, you are the face of NexaCore. Professionalism in communication, attire, and behavior is mandatory.
- **Communication Tools:** Use corporate communication channels (Email, Slack, Teams) for professional purposes. Avoid using these tools for personal disputes or non-work-related content that may be deemed inappropriate.

For a detailed breakdown of behavioral expectations, a list of prohibited actions, and reporting mechanisms, please refer to the **Code of Conduct (HR-006)**.

### 3.2 Working Hours and Attendance Principles
NexaCore follows a flexible but structured approach to working hours to accommodate our global delivery model and various time zones.
- **Standard Hours:** The core business hours for the Lucknow HQ are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST. Employees in other regions follow their local core hours.
- **Core Availability:** While we offer flexibility, employees are expected to be available and responsive during their core hours as agreed upon with their Reporting Manager.
- **Punctuality:** Consistent attendance is essential. Whether in the office or remote, arriving at meetings on time and meeting project deadlines is expected.
- **Overtime:** Due to the nature of consulting, occasional overtime may be required to meet critical client deadlines. Please coordinate any significant overtime with your Reporting Manager to ensure a healthy work-life balance.

### 3.3 Attendance Monitoring and Tracking
Attendance and time-tracking are managed via the internal HR portal.
- **Daily Status:** Employees should update their status (e.g., Available, In a Meeting, Out of Office) on company communication tools.
- **Absence Reporting:** All planned absences, including Annual Leave, must be logged in the HR portal for approval.
- **Unplanned Absences:** In case of sudden illness or emergency, notify your Reporting Manager as soon as possible.

## 4. Employee Responsibilities

### 4.1 Performance and Continuous Growth
We believe that the growth of the company is tied to the growth of our people. Every employee is responsible for their own professional development.
- **KPI Alignment:** Work with your Reporting Manager to establish clear, measurable goals at the start of the fiscal year.
- **Self-Driven Learning:** Proactively identify new technologies or methodologies that could benefit NexaCore or our clients.
- **Feedback Loop:** Actively seek feedback during and between formal review cycles to improve your performance.

Refer to the **Performance Review Policy (HR-005)** for details on the review cycle, rating principles, and the performance improvement process.

### 4.2 Professional Communication
Effective communication is the backbone of our hybrid operations. Employees are expected to:
- **Responsive Communication:** Respond to internal communications within a reasonable timeframe. Non-urgent emails should be acknowledged within 24 hours; Slack/Teams messages should be acknowledged within a few business hours.
- **Transparency:** Be honest about project status, including risks and delays. It is always better to report a potential delay early than to miss a deadline without warning.
- **Professionalism in Writing:** All external communications (emails, reports, presentations) must be polished, grammatically correct, and aligned with NexaCore's brand guidelines.

### 4.3 Confidentiality and Data Stewardship
As a technology consulting firm, we handle highly sensitive client data and internal strategic information. This is a critical responsibility.
- **NDA Compliance:** All employees must strictly adhere to the Non-Disclosure Agreement signed upon joining.
- **Data Minimization:** Only access the client data and internal documents necessary for your current task.
- **Secure Handling:** Never share passwords, API keys, or client credentials via unencrypted channels like Slack or personal email.
- **Reporting Breaches:** If you suspect that confidential information has been leaked or accessed by an unauthorized person, you must report this as a **Security Incident** to the IT and Security department immediately.

## 6. Information Security Responsibilities

Every employee, regardless of their role or seniority, is a steward of NexaCore's security. Basic responsibilities include:
- **Credential Management:** Using strong, unique passwords and ensuring that Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is enabled on all corporate accounts.
- **Workstation Security:** Locking your computer screen when leaving your desk (even at home) and ensuring that all OS and software updates are applied promptly.
- **Secure Networking:** Using the company-approved VPN for all access to internal servers, cloud consoles, and client environments.
- **Physical Asset Care:** Ensuring that company-issued laptops and devices are stored securely and not left unattended in public spaces.

For comprehensive security rules and the full incident response framework, refer to the **Information Security Policy (ITSEC-001)**.

## 7. Support, Resources, and Help

### 7.1 Where to Seek Help
If you have questions regarding the content of this handbook, company policies, or your employment terms, please reach out to the following:
- **Policy and Benefits Clarifications:** Contact your local HR representative or email `hr@nexacore.com`.
- **Technical Support and Security:** Open a ticket via the IT portal or contact the IT and Security team.
- **Management and Career Concerns:** Discuss issues with your Reporting Manager. If the issue involves your manager, escalate to the next level of leadership or the HR department.

### 7.2 The HR Portal
The internal HR portal is the central hub for all employee self-service actions:
- **Leave Management:** Apply for and track your Annual and Sick Leave.
- **Profile Management:** Update your contact information and emergency contacts.
- **Payroll and Tax:** Access your monthly payslips and annual tax documents.
- **Policy Library:** Review the latest versions of all company policies and SOPs.
