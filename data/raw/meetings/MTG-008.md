# Meeting Summary
The budget planning meeting addressed the overspend in the Engineering department due to Project Orion's GPU requirements. A budget reallocation of ₹200,000 from the Operations travel budget was approved to offset these costs. The team also addressed an invoice discrepancy with Bluewave Cloud Services (VEN-014) and reinforced the strict adherence to the Procurement Policy (FIN-001) and Expense Reimbursement Policy (FIN-003).

# Decisions Taken
1. **Budget Reallocation:** ₹200,000 moved from Operations travel to Engineering compute.
2. **Vendor Payment:** Payment for INV-2026-0108 is held until a revised PO is issued to cover the "Premium Support" charge.
3. **Expense Enforcement:** Strict enforcement of the 30-day submission window for expense claims; late submissions will be rejected.

# Open Issues
1. **GPU Cost Normalization:** The long-term cost of the reranker is still uncertain and depends on the success of the semantic cache.
2. **Legacy Vendor Contracts:** Need to review all other vendor contracts to ensure no other "hidden" support charges exist.
