# Information Security Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish the overarching framework for protecting the information assets of NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. As a technology consulting and managed IT services organization, NexaCore holds sensitive client data, proprietary intellectual property, and confidential business information across multiple geographies, including Lucknow, Bengaluru, Dubai, and Singapore. This policy defines the principles, responsibilities, and minimum security controls that every employee, contractor, and third-party partner must adhere to in order to safeguard these assets from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

This policy serves as the foundational security document for the organization. All other security policies, including the Password Policy (ITSEC-002), Acceptable Use Policy (ITSEC-003), Data Classification Policy (ITSEC-006), and VPN & Remote Access Policy (ITSEC-008), derive their authority from and must remain consistent with the principles established here.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees of NexaCore Solutions globally, including full-time, part-time, and contractual staff, as well as interns, consultants, and any third-party personnel who access NexaCore systems, networks, or data. The policy covers all information assets owned or managed by the company, including but not limited to:

- **Hardware:** Laptops, desktops, servers, mobile devices, and peripherals.
- **Software:** Licensed applications, internally developed tools, and cloud services.
- **Data:** Client data, source code, financial records, employee records, and business plans.
- **Networks:** Corporate networks, VPN infrastructure, and cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP).

## 3. Information Security Governance

### 3.1 Leadership and Accountability
The Head of IT & Security, **Rohit Verma** (CISO), holds overall accountability for the design, implementation, and continuous improvement of NexaCore's information security program. The CISO reports directly to the CEO, **Arvind Malhotra**, and works in close coordination with the Head of HR, **Farah Hussain**, and the CFO, **Kabir Nair**, to ensure that security controls are aligned with business objectives, regulatory requirements, and financial constraints.

### 3.2 Security Committee
A cross-functional Security Committee, chaired by the CISO, meets quarterly to review security metrics, approve policy changes, and prioritize remediation efforts. The committee includes representatives from Engineering, Operations, Finance, and Human Resources to ensure that security decisions consider the operational realities of each department.

## 4. Core Security Principles

### 4.1 Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA)
All security controls at NexaCore are designed to uphold the CIA triad:
- **Confidentiality:** Information is accessible only to those authorized to view it, as determined by the Data Classification Policy (ITSEC-006).
- **Integrity:** Information is accurate, complete, and protected from unauthorized modification.
- **Availability:** Information and systems are accessible to authorized users when needed for legitimate business purposes.

### 4.2 Defense in Depth
NexaCore employs multiple layers of security controls so that the failure of any single control does not compromise the overall security posture. These layers include physical security, network segmentation, endpoint protection, identity and access management, encryption, and continuous monitoring.

### 4.3 Least Privilege
Access to systems and data is granted on a need-to-know basis. Employees are granted the minimum level of access required to perform their job functions. Any request for elevated privileges must be justified, approved by the appropriate authority, and reviewed periodically.

## 5. Employee Responsibilities
Every employee of NexaCore is a steward of the company's information assets and bears personal responsibility for their protection. Specific responsibilities include:

- **Compliance:** Adhering to all security policies and reporting any known or suspected violations to the IT and Security department.
- **Credential Security:** Protecting passwords, tokens, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) credentials. Passwords must never be shared, written down in plain sight, or reused across personal and corporate accounts. Refer to the Password Policy (ITSEC-002) for detailed requirements.
- **Device Care:** Ensuring that company-issued devices are physically secure and not left unattended in public spaces.
- **Incident Reporting:** Immediately reporting any suspected **Security Incident**, including phishing attempts, malware infections, lost devices, or unusual system behavior, to the IT and Security team. Refer to the Incident Response Plan (ITSEC-005) for the reporting procedure.
- **Data Handling:** Handling all information in accordance with its classification level, as defined in the Data Classification Policy (ITSEC-006).

## 6. Acceptable Use of Company Resources
Company-provided systems, networks, and data are intended for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted where it does not interfere with productivity, consume excessive resources, or violate any law or policy. Employees must review the Acceptable Use Policy (ITSEC-003) for the complete set of permitted and prohibited activities.

## 7. Remote Work and Access Security
NexaCore supports flexible working arrangements, including **Remote Work** as defined in the Remote Work Policy (HR-003). All remote access to corporate resources must be conducted through the company-approved VPN, as detailed in the VPN & Remote Access Policy (ITSEC-008). Direct access to internal systems over unsecured public networks is strictly prohibited.

## 8. Data Protection and Privacy

### 8.1 Client Data
NexaCore handles sensitive client data as part of its consulting and managed services engagements. Employees must treat all client data with the highest level of care and comply with any contractual or regulatory obligations associated with that data. Client data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.

### 8.2 Personal Data
NexaCore collects and processes personal data of its employees in accordance with applicable data protection laws. The HR department, under the leadership of **Farah Hussain**, is responsible for ensuring that employee personal data is collected, stored, and processed lawfully and transparently.

## 9. Physical Security
Physical access to NexaCore offices and data centers is controlled through badge access and visitor management procedures. Employees must:
- Wear their identification badges at all times while on company premises.
- Escort visitors at all times and report any unescorted visitors to Administration.
- Ensure that sensitive documents and screens are not visible to unauthorized individuals.

## 10. Third-Party and Vendor Security
Third-party vendors and partners who require access to NexaCore systems or data must undergo a security assessment before access is granted. Vendor contracts must include appropriate confidentiality and data protection clauses. The Procurement Policy (FIN-001) governs the vendor onboarding process, and the IT and Security department must be consulted for any vendor engagement involving access to sensitive systems or data.

## 11. Security Awareness and Training
All employees must complete mandatory security awareness training upon joining NexaCore and annually thereafter. Training covers phishing awareness, password hygiene, data classification, incident reporting, and remote work security. The HR department coordinates training schedules, while the IT and Security department develops and delivers the content.

## 12. Policy Compliance and Enforcement
Compliance with this policy is mandatory. Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, in accordance with the Code of Conduct (HR-006) and the Employee Exit Procedure (HR-007). In cases involving criminal activity, NexaCore will cooperate fully with law enforcement authorities.
