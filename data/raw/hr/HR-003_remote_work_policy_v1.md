# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose
NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. recognizes that flexible work arrangements can enhance productivity, support employee well-being, and attract top talent from diverse geographies. We believe that work is an activity, not a place. However, we also recognize that the synergy of in-person collaboration is vital for innovation and team cohesion, especially in a complex technology consulting environment. This policy defines the framework for **Remote Work**, ensuring that flexibility is balanced with the need for collaboration, security, and operational excellence.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees of NexaCore Solutions whose roles are designated as "Hybrid" or "Remote-Eligible" by their department head. Roles that require physical presence for hardware maintenance, site-specific administration, or high-security physical access are excluded from this policy.

## 3. Remote Work Framework

### 3.1 Standard Remote Work Limit
The standard arrangement for hybrid employees at NexaCore is a maximum of **2 days per week of Remote Work**. 
- **In-Office Requirement:** The remaining 3 days of the working week must be spent at the employee's designated office location (Lucknow, Bengaluru, Dubai, or Singapore).
- **Coordination:** The specific days of Remote Work should be coordinated with and approved by the **Reporting Manager** to ensure sufficient team overlap for brainstorming, sprint planning, and critical project meetings.
- **Flexibility:** While the 2-day limit is the standard, managers may occasionally allow an additional remote day for a specific week to accommodate short-term personal needs, provided it does not impact delivery.

### 3.2 Extended Remote Work
In exceptional circumstances, an employee may request **Extended Remote Work**, which is defined as any arrangement where the employee works remotely for more than 2 days per week on a consistent basis.
- **Eligible Scenarios:** Extended Remote Work may be granted for reasons such as significant personal/family hardship, medical necessity, or strategic business needs where the employee is located far from a NexaCore hub.
- **Approval Process:** Extended Remote Work is not a right; it is a conditional arrangement. It requires formal, joint sign-off from both the **Reporting Manager** and the **HR department**.
- **Review Cycle:** To ensure that the arrangement is still meeting business needs and that the employee is not feeling isolated, all Extended Remote Work arrangements must be **reviewed quarterly**. The review will assess project performance, communication effectiveness, and the employee's well-being.
- **Documentation:** All approved extended arrangements must be officially documented as a lapped agreement in the employee's file within the HR portal.

## 4. Eligibility and Approval Process

### 4.1 Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for any form of Remote Work, an employee must meet the following criteria:
- **Probation Status:** Must have successfully completed the initial probation period and received a positive performance confirmation.
- **Performance Track Record:** Must demonstrate a consistent ability to meet KPIs and deliverables without constant direct supervision.
- **Infrastructure:** Must possess a secure, quiet home environment with reliable high-speed internet capable of supporting high-definition video conferencing.
- **Role Suitability:** The role must be one that can be performed effectively using digital collaboration tools without requiring physical access to hardware or specialized on-site equipment.

### 4.2 Request and Authorization Workflow
1. **Submission:** The employee submits a Remote Work request via the HR portal, specifying their proposed remote days.
2. **Managerial Review:** The Reporting Manager assesses the request based on project requirements, team dynamics, and the employee's ability to work independently.
3. **Notification:** Approval or denial is communicated through the portal. Denials must include a justification (e.g., "Required for on-site client workshops").

## 5. Employee Responsibilities and Expectations

### 5.1 Availability and Digital Presence
Remote Work is a shift in location, not a shift in expectations. Employees working remotely must:
- **Core Hours:** Be fully available and responsive during the core business hours of their assigned office.
- **Communication:** Maintain a "presence" on company communication tools (Slack, Teams). Use status updates (e.g., "Focused Work," "Lunch Break") to keep the team informed.
- **Virtual Meetings:** Attend all scheduled virtual meetings. Cameras should be enabled by default to foster a sense of connection and engagement.
- **Reporting:** Provide a clear daily or weekly progress summary to their Reporting Manager, highlighting achievements and any blockers encountered.

### 5.2 Performance Standards
Performance expectations remain identical regardless of the work location. 
- **Deliverables:** Work is measured by output and outcomes, not by hours spent at a desk.
- **Responsiveness:** A failure to be responsive during core hours while working remotely may be viewed as an unauthorized absence and may lead to the revocation of remote work privileges.

## 6. Information Security and Equipment

### 6.1 Security Expectations and Obligations
Working remotely introduces new security vectors. All employees must adhere to the following strictly:
- **Secure Connection:** Use the company-approved VPN for all access to internal servers, cloud consoles, and client environments. Direct access to corporate resources over public internet is strictly prohibited.
- **Network Privacy:** Never use public, unsecured Wi-Fi (e.g., in cafes or airports) for corporate work. Use a secure home network or a company-authorized personal hotspot.
- **Device Integrity:** Only company-issued devices may be used to access corporate data. The use of personal laptops or tablets for work is prohibited unless explicitly authorized by IT and Security for a specific use case.
- **Physical Privacy:** Ensure that screens are not visible to non-employees when working in shared spaces. Avoid discussing sensitive client projects in public areas.

For comprehensive security rules and a detailed list of prohibited actions, refer to the **Information Security Policy (ITSEC-001)**.

### 6.2 Equipment Provisioning and Care
- **Provisioning:** NexaCore provides a standard remote-work kit, including a high-performance laptop, mouse, keyboard, and headset.
- **Maintenance:** Employees are responsible for the physical care and security of company-issued equipment.
- **Technical Support:** Remote employees should report all hardware or software issues via the IT ticket system.
- **Asset Return:** All equipment must be returned immediately upon termination of employment or if the Remote Work arrangement is revoked. Refer to the **Employee Exit Procedure (HR-007)** for the return process.

## 7. Exceptions and Revocation

### 7.1 Revocation of Remote Work Privileges
The company reserves the right to revoke an employee's remote work privileges if:
- **Performance Decline:** There is a measurable drop in the quality or timeliness of deliverables.
- **Behavioral Issues:** The employee fails to remain responsive or engage with the team during remote days.
- **Security Breach:** The employee violates a security policy (e.g., using a non-VPN connection).
- **Business Necessity:** The role's requirements change, requiring a full-time office presence.

### 7.2 Managerial Overrides
Reporting Managers may request a "Mandatory Office Day" for any employee, regardless of their usual remote schedule, to accommodate critical workshops, client visits, or emergency team-building events.
