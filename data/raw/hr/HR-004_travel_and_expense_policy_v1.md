# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to provide clear, comprehensive guidelines for business-related travel and the reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred by employees of NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. Our goal is to ensure that employees are not financially burdened by their professional duties while maintaining strict fiscal responsibility, transparency, and alignment with corporate budgeting.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and authorized representatives of NexaCore Solutions who travel on official company business, regardless of their location or seniority.

## 3. General Principles
- **Advance Authorization:** All business travel must be authorized in writing before any bookings are made.
- **Prudent Spending:** Employees are expected to exercise professional judgment and seek the most cost-effective options that do not compromise safety, efficiency, or the professional image of the company.
- **Actual Cost Reimbursement:** NexaCore operates on an "actual cost" basis. This means the company reimburses legitimate expenses supported by receipts, rather than providing a flat-rate allowance.

## 4. Business Travel Authorization

### 4.1 Requesting Travel
Before making any travel arrangements, employees must submit a formal Travel Request via the Finance portal. This request is essential for budget tracking and insurance purposes. The request must include:
- **Purpose of Trip:** A detailed explanation of the business objective (e.g., "On-site deployment for Client X").
- **Destination and Duration:** The specific city/country and the total number of days.
- **Estimated Cost Breakdown:** a projected cost for airfare, hotel, and per diem meals.
- **Project Code:** The specific client or internal project code to which the costs will be billed.

### 4.2 Approval Tiers
- **Domestic Travel (within India):** Requires approval from the employee's **Reporting Manager**.
- **Regional Travel (within MEA or APAC):** Requires approval from the Reporting Manager AND the respective Regional Manager (e.g., Daniel Lim for APAC).
- **International Travel (cross-region):** Requires approval from the Reporting Manager AND the relevant Department Head (e.g., VP Engineering or VP Sales).
- **Emergency Travel:** In cases of extreme urgency, verbal approval may be granted by a manager, but the formal request must be filed in the portal within 48 hours.

## 5. Eligible and Non-Reimbursable Expenses

### 5.1 Reimbursable Expenses
The following expenses are generally considered legitimate and are eligible for **Expense Reimbursement**:

#### 5.1.1 Transportation
- **Airfare:** Economy class is the standard for all flights. Premium Economy may be approved for flights exceeding 6 hours if justified by the Reporting Manager. Business class is reserved exclusively for C-level executives.
- **Local Transport:** Taxis, approved ride-sharing services (e.g., Uber, Lyft), and public transport used specifically for business transit.
- **Car Rentals:** Mid-sized vehicles for business purposes, provided they are the most economical choice for the destination.

#### 5.1.2 Lodging
- **Hotels:** Standard rooms at business-class hotels. Luxury or suite bookings are not permitted without explicit justification and VP-level approval.
- **Short-Term Rentals:** Approved corporate apartments for long-term assignments.

#### 5.1.3 Meals and Incidentals
- **Daily Meals:** Reasonable costs for breakfast, lunch, and dinner while traveling.
- **Client Entertainment:** Business meals with clients, provided the purpose is documented and the cost is reasonable.

### 5.2 Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following expenses are considered personal and will not be reimbursed by NexaCore:
- **Personal Amenities:** Laundry, dry cleaning, or spa services (unless the trip exceeds 10 days and is approved).
- **In-Flight Luxury:** Movies, premium Wi-Fi, or alcohol purchased during flights.
- **Personal Entertainment:** Sightseeing, tours, or movie tickets during business trips.
- **Legal Penalties:** Traffic fines, parking tickets, or other legal penalties incurred during travel.
- **Guests:** Any expenses related to spouses, partners, or guests accompanying the employee.
- **Personal Toiletries:** All toiletries and personal care items.

## 6. Expense Reimbursement Process

### 6.1 Submission Deadline
Timely submission is critical for accurate financial reporting and project billing. All **Expense Reimbursement** claims must be submitted through the Finance portal **within 30 days of the expense date**.
- **Late Submissions:** Claims submitted after the 30-day window may be rejected. If a legitimate reason for the delay exists, an exception must be approved by the Department Head.
- **Fiscal Month End:** All expenses must be filed by the end of the fiscal month in which they occurred to ensure they are billed to the correct project period.

### 6.2 Documentation and Evidence
No claim will be processed without proper evidence. Every reimbursement request must include:
- **Itemized Receipts:** Digital scans or high-quality photos of original itemized receipts. Credit card statements are not acceptable substitutes for itemized bills for expenses exceeding ₹1,000.
- **Business Justification:** A brief description of the expense and its direct relation to a specific business objective.
- **Attendee Log:** For all client entertainment expenses, a list of all attendees (name and organization) must be provided.

### 6.3 Approval Workflow
1. **Submission:** The employee uploads all receipts and submits the claim via the portal.
2. **Managerial Review:** The Reporting Manager verifies that the expenses are legitimate, within the travel budget, and aligned with the approved trip purpose.
3. **Finance Audit:** The Finance department audits the claim for policy compliance, tax validity (GST/VAT), and correct project coding.
4. **Payment:** Once fully approved, the reimbursement is processed as a separate payment or added to the next payroll cycle.

## 7. Travel Advances
For long-term international assignments or high-cost projects, employees may request a travel advance to avoid personal financial strain.
- **Application:** Requests must be submitted via the Finance portal 14 days prior to the departure date.
- **Settlement:** Advances must be settled by submitting a complete expense report within 15 days of returning from the trip.
- **Return of Funds:** Any unused portion of the advance must be returned to the Finance department immediately upon settlement.

## 8. International Travel and Regulatory Compliance
- **Visas and Passports:** Employees are responsible for ensuring they have a valid passport. NexaCore will reimburse the cost of necessary business visas.
- **Insurance:** All international travelers are covered by the company's corporate travel insurance. Employees must carry their insurance card at all times.
- **Currency Exchange:** Expenses should be claimed in the original currency of expenditure. The Finance portal will automatically apply the exchange rate based on the transaction date.

## 9. Exceptions and Appeals
The CFO (Kabir Nair) has the final authority to approve exceptions to this policy, such as higher-tier travel for medical reasons or emergency expenditures that exceed standard limits.
