# Meeting Summary
The DR simulation revealed a critical failure in the recovery of the hybrid retrieval pipeline. While the primary data center failover to Bengaluru worked, the BM25 sparse index was not replicated, leading to a two-hour recovery time and a violation of the RTO (30 mins). The dense vector store also showed a 15-minute RPO lag. The team identified gaps in ITSEC-007 and the need for a structured incident communication protocol.

# Decisions Taken
1. **Index Replication:** All search indices (dense and sparse) must now be replicated to the secondary site with a maximum lag of 5 minutes.
2. **Policy Update:** ITSEC-007 will be updated to explicitly require the replication of search indices, not just source data.
3. **Communication Protocol:** Establish a dedicated "Incident Command" Slack channel and Zoom bridge for all P0 incidents.

# Open Issues
1. **BM25 Replication Method:** The team needs to decide between a snapshot-and-ship approach or migrating to a distributed search engine.
2. **RPO Reduction:** The 15-minute lag in the dense index still exceeds the desired 5-minute RPO.
