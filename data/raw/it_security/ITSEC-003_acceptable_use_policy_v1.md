# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to define the acceptable and responsible use of NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd.'s information technology resources, including computers, networks, email, internet access, software, and data. NexaCore provides these resources to enable employees to perform their job functions effectively and to support the company's business objectives. This policy establishes clear expectations for how these resources may be used, protects the company from legal and security risks, and ensures that all users understand the boundaries of acceptable behavior.

This policy is subordinate to and must be read in conjunction with the Information Security Policy (ITSEC-001), which establishes the overarching security framework for the organization.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all users of NexaCore's IT resources, including:
- All full-time, part-time, and contractual employees.
- Interns and trainees.
- Consultants and third-party contractors.
- Any external party granted access to NexaCore systems.

The policy covers all company-provided devices, including laptops, desktops, mobile phones, tablets, and any other equipment connected to the corporate network, as well as the use of personal devices to access corporate resources where permitted.

## 3. General Principles

### 3.1 Business Purpose
NexaCore's IT resources are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it:
- Does not interfere with the employee's job performance or productivity.
- Does not consume excessive network bandwidth or storage resources.
- Does not violate any law, regulation, or company policy.
- Does not expose the company to security or reputational risk.

### 3.2 No Expectation of Privacy
Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company-provided IT resources. NexaCore reserves the right to monitor, record, review, and audit all activity on its systems and networks, including email, internet usage, file access, and application usage, to the extent permitted by applicable law. This monitoring is conducted to protect the company's assets, ensure compliance with policies, and investigate potential security incidents.

## 4. Acceptable Use of Company Devices

### 4.1 Device Ownership and Care
All company-issued devices remain the property of NexaCore Solutions. Employees are responsible for:
- **Physical Security:** Keeping devices secure and not leaving them unattended in public places.
- **Software Installation:** Installing only company-approved software. Unauthorized software installation is prohibited.
- **Updates:** Ensuring that operating system and security updates are installed promptly as directed by the IT and Security department.
- **Reporting:** Reporting any loss, theft, or suspected compromise of a company device to the IT and Security department immediately.

### 4.2 Personal Use of Company Devices
Limited personal use of company devices is permitted, subject to the following restrictions:
- Personal use must not interfere with work responsibilities.
- Personal files must not be stored on company devices in a way that consumes excessive storage.
- Personal use must not involve illegal activities, gambling, or access to inappropriate content.

## 5. Email and Communication

### 5.1 Professional Conduct
All email and communication sent through company systems must be professional, courteous, and consistent with the Code of Conduct (HR-006). Employees must:
- Use appropriate language and tone in all communications.
- Not send emails that are harassing, discriminatory, or offensive.
- Not use company email for personal financial transactions or subscriptions unless approved.

### 5.2 Email Security
Employees must exercise caution when using email to protect against phishing and malware:
- **Unknown Senders:** Do not open attachments or click links in emails from unknown or unexpected senders.
- **Verification:** Verify the authenticity of unusual requests, particularly those involving financial transfers or sensitive data, through a secondary communication channel.
- **Sensitive Data:** Do not send data classified as `restricted` or `confidential` under the Data Classification Policy (ITSEC-006) via unencrypted email.
- **Forwarding:** Do not forward confidential or proprietary information to personal email addresses or external parties without authorization.

### 5.3 Email Retention
Corporate email is a business record and may be subject to legal discovery. Employees must not delete emails that are relevant to ongoing projects, legal matters, or regulatory requirements. Refer to the Data Classification Policy (ITSEC-006) for retention requirements.

## 6. Internet and Web Usage

### 6.1 Permitted Use
Internet access is provided to support business operations. Employees may use the internet for:
- Research related to their job functions.
- Accessing client portals and cloud services.
- Professional development and training.
- Limited personal browsing that does not interfere with work.

### 6.2 Prohibited Use
The following internet activities are strictly prohibited:
- **Illegal Content:** Accessing, downloading, or distributing illegal content, including pirated software, copyrighted material without authorization, or content that violates applicable laws.
- **Inappropriate Content:** Accessing pornographic, violent, or otherwise inappropriate content.
- **Gambling:** Accessing online gambling or betting sites.
- **Peer-to-Peer File Sharing:** Using peer-to-peer file-sharing applications to download or distribute files, unless explicitly approved for business purposes.
- **Bypassing Controls:** Attempting to bypass network security controls, firewalls, or content filters.

### 6.3 Social Media
Employees may use social media for professional purposes, such as networking and promoting NexaCore's services, provided they:
- Do not disclose confidential or proprietary information.
- Do not represent themselves as speaking on behalf of NexaCore without authorization.
- Do not post content that could damage the company's reputation.
- Comply with the Code of Conduct (HR-006) regarding professional behavior.

## 7. Software and Licensing

### 7.1 Licensed Software
Employees may only use software that is properly licensed by NexaCore. The installation of unlicensed or pirated software is strictly prohibited. The IT and Security department maintains an inventory of approved software and manages software licensing.

### 7.2 Open Source Software
The use of open-source software is permitted where it is approved by the IT and Security department and complies with applicable license terms. Employees must not incorporate open-source code into proprietary deliverables without reviewing the license implications, as this could create legal obligations for the company.

### 7.3 Cloud Services
Employees must not subscribe to or use cloud services (e.g., personal Dropbox, Google Drive, or other file-sharing services) to store or transmit corporate data without approval. All corporate data must be stored in company-approved cloud environments. Refer to the Data Classification Policy (ITSEC-006) for data storage requirements. Financial data and invoice records are managed under the Finance department's mandate, and access to these systems is governed by the Procurement Policy (FIN-001).

## 8. Data Handling and Confidentiality

### 8.1 Data Classification
All data must be handled in accordance with its classification level, as defined in the Data Classification Policy (ITSEC-006). Employees must:
- Not store `restricted` or `confidential` data on personal devices or personal cloud storage.
- Not transmit sensitive data over unsecured networks.
- Use encryption when required by the data's classification.

### 8.2 Client Data
Client data is among NexaCore's most valuable assets. Employees must:
- Access client data only as required for their job functions.
- Not copy client data to personal devices or personal accounts.
- Comply with all client-specific data handling requirements.

## 9. Remote Work and Personal Devices

### 9.1 Remote Work
Employees working remotely must comply with the Remote Work Policy (HR-003) and the VPN & Remote Access Policy (ITSEC-008). All remote access to corporate resources must be conducted through the company-approved VPN.

### 9.2 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
NexaCore generally prohibits the use of personal devices to access corporate data, except where explicitly authorized by the IT and Security department for a specific use case. Where BYOD is authorized, the device must:
- Have a screen lock enabled.
- Run a current operating system with security updates installed.
- Not be rooted or jailbroken.
- Comply with any additional requirements specified by the IT and Security department.

## 10. Monitoring and Enforcement

### 10.1 Monitoring
NexaCore may monitor user activity on its systems and networks to:
- Ensure compliance with this policy and other company policies.
- Detect and respond to security threats.
- Investigate suspected misconduct or policy violations.
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

### 10.2 Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in:
- Revocation of access privileges.
- Disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, in accordance with the Code of Conduct (HR-006).
- Legal action where the violation constitutes a criminal offense or causes harm to the company.

Security incidents involving violations of this policy will be handled under the Incident Response Plan (ITSEC-005).
