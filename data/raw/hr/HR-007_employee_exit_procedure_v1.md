# Employee Exit Procedure

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to ensure a professional, secure, and orderly transition when an employee leaves NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. A structured exit process is critical for protecting the company's intellectual property, ensuring the seamless return of corporate assets, and providing the departing employee with a clear, transparent path to final administrative clearance and settlement.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all employees of NexaCore Solutions, regardless of the reason for departure, including voluntary resignation, retirement, or termination by the company.

## 3. Exit Initiation and Notification

### 3.1 Voluntary Resignation
Employees wishing to resign from their position are required to follow a formal process to allow the company to plan for the transition.
- **Resignation Letter:** Submit a formal resignation letter via the HR portal. The letter must be addressed to the employee's **Reporting Manager** and the HR department.
- **Last Working Day:** The letter must clearly specify the intended last working day.

### 3.2 Notice Period
The notice period is the mandatory duration an employee is expected to remain with the company after submitting their resignation to ensure an effective handover of duties.
- **Notice Duration:** The specific notice period is defined in the individual's signed employment contract.
- **Modification:** Notice periods may be waived or shortened by mutual agreement between the Reporting Manager and the HR department, typically if the employee's role is not critical to current deliveries.

### 3.3 Involuntary Termination
In cases of termination by the company (e.g., redundancy or termination for cause), the exit process is initiated by the HR department and the relevant Department Head.
- **Notification:** The employee will be notified of their final date and the specific terms of their departure in a formal letter.
- **Immediate Exit:** In cases of gross misconduct, the company may terminate employment immediately, with all access revoked instantly.

## 4. Responsibilities During the Transition Period

### 4.1 The Reporting Manager's Responsibilities
The Reporting Manager's primary goal is to ensure business continuity and prevent the loss of critical institutional knowledge.
- **Handover Planning:** Create a comprehensive list of all active projects, pending tasks, and administrative responsibilities that must be transitioned.
- **Knowledge Transfer (KT) Sessions:** Schedule and oversee a series of KT sessions between the exiting employee and the designated successor.
- **Documentation Review:** Ensure that all project documentation, code comments, and process guides are updated and uploaded to the company's shared repositories.
- **Client Transition:** Coordinate with the employee to inform clients of the change in a professional manner, ensuring the client feels supported during the transition.

### 4.2 The Employee's Responsibilities
The exiting employee is expected to maintain the same level of professionalism and productivity until their final working hour.
- **Task Completion:** Complete all pending deliverables to the best of their ability.
- **Active KT Participation:** Lead the Knowledge Transfer sessions and ensure the successor is fully equipped to take over the role.
- **File Organization:** Organize all digital files, emails, and documents, ensuring that no critical corporate information is stored on personal drives or in local folders.
- **Professionalism:** Maintain a positive and supportive attitude toward the team during the exit period.

## 5. Asset Return and IT De-provisioning

### 5.1 Corporate Asset Return
Before final clearance is granted, all corporate assets must be returned to the IT and Security department.
- **Physical Hardware:** This includes laptops, monitors, keyboards, headsets, mobile phones, and any other specialized peripherals.
- **Security Credentials:** All physical badges, office key cards, and physical keys must be returned.
- **Verification Process:** The IT department will perform a physical inspection of the hardware to ensure it is in acceptable condition and that no components are missing.

Refer to the **IT Asset Management Policy (ITSEC-004)** for the detailed asset tracking and return specifications.

### 5.2 IT Access Termination
To protect NexaCore's and its clients' data, access is revoked according to the following schedule:
- **Standard Exit:** Access to email, Slack, VPN, and cloud consoles (AWS, Azure, GCP) is revoked at the close of business on the employee's last working day.
- **Immediate Exit:** For terminations for cause, all access is revoked the moment the termination meeting concludes.
- **Post-Exit Audit:** The IT and Security team will conduct a final audit of the employee's activity logs to ensure that no sensitive corporate data was inappropriately exported shortly before departure.

Refer to the **Information Security Policy (ITSEC-001)** for the legal framework and technical requirements surrounding data protection upon employment exit.

## 6. Administrative Clearance and Final Settlement

### 6.1 The Final Clearance Checklist
An employee is not considered "cleared" for departure until the final checklist is completed and signed off by the following authorities:
- **Reporting Manager:** Confirming that all knowledge transfer is complete and all deliverables have been handed over.
- **IT and Security:** Confirming the return of all physical assets and the successful revocation of all digital access.
- **Finance:** Confirming that there are no outstanding travel advances, loans, or other financial obligations to the company.
- **HR:** Confirming that all administrative requirements (e.g., return of ID badges) have been met.

### 6.2 Final Settlement (Full and Final - F&F)
Once full clearance is obtained, the Finance department will process the final settlement.
- **Salary and Benefits:** Payment of the final month's salary and any accrued bonuses.
- **Leave Encashment:** Payment for eligible unused Annual Leave, as per the terms in the **Leave Policy (HR-002)** and local labor laws.
- **Deductions:** Any outstanding debts or the cost of unreturned assets will be deducted from the final settlement.

## 7. Exit Interview Process
NexaCore views the departure of an employee as an opportunity to improve the organization.
- **The Interview:** HR will schedule a confidential exit interview during the employee's final week.
- **Objective:** To gather honest feedback regarding the employee's experience, the effectiveness of their management, and suggestions for organizational improvement.
- **Confidentiality:** The feedback is aggregated and reported to leadership to drive systemic changes, while protecting the identity of the departing employee where requested.

## 8. Post-Employment Obligations

### 8.1 Continuing Confidentiality
Departure from NexaCore does not terminate the legal obligation to protect company secrets.
- **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA):** The NDA signed at the start of employment remains in effect indefinitely. The unauthorized disclosure of client data or internal strategies after departure is a legal violation.
- **Non-Solicitation:** Any non-solicitation agreements regarding clients or other employees must be honored as per the specific terms of the employment contract.
