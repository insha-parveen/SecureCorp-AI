Hi Sunita and Ayesha,

The Password Policy (ITSEC-002) is now effective as of January 15, 2026. Key requirements:

- Minimum 12-character passwords
- MFA mandatory for all systems
- Password rotation every 90 days (non-MFA) / 180 days (MFA)
- Account lockout after 5 failed attempts
- No password reuse (last 5)

Please ensure your teams are aware. The IT team will enforce these controls across all systems. Refer to ITSEC-001 for the overarching security framework.
