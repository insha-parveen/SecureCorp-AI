Hi Sunita and Ayesha,

The Data Classification Policy (ITSEC-006) is now published. This policy establishes four classification levels:

- Public: No special handling
- Internal: All employees, no external sharing
- Restricted: Need-to-know, encrypted, no personal devices
- Confidential: Named individuals, strong encryption, secure destruction

All data must be handled according to its classification. Please ensure your teams apply the appropriate labels.
